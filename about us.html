Our Mission & History

Established in 1984, Siyabonga Africa is committed to ending poverty in South Africa by equipping individuals with valuable, market-aligned skills.

We work directly with disadvantaged communities to foster self-reliance, build sustainable livelihoods, and nurture micro-entrepreneurs.

Our Core Values

Empowerment

Giving individuals the practical skills and confidence needed to build their own futures.

Integrity

Maintaining full transparency and financial accountability as a registered Non-Profit Organisation.

Sustainability

Designing community interventions that create long-term economic independence.
